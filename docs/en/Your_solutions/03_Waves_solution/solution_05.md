# Task 05 – Echo Ranging

## Problem Statement

A person shouts towards a cliff and hears the echo after $1  \text{s}$. The speed of sound in air is $v = 343  \text{m/s}$.

Determine the distance to the cliff.

---

## Theory

An echo occurs when a sound wave reflects off a surface and returns to the source. The total distance traveled by the sound is twice the distance to the reflecting surface:

$$
d_{\text{total}} = 2d
$$

The relation between distance, speed, and time is:

$$
d_{\text{total}} = v . t
$$

Thus, the distance to the cliff is:

$$
d = \frac{v . t}{2}
$$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

Given:

$$
v = 343 \text{ m/s} \quad t = 1 \text{ s}
$$

Use the formula:

$$
d = \frac{v . t}{2}
$$

Substitute values:

$$
d = \frac{343 \cdot 1}{2}
$$

$$
d = 171.5 \text{ m}
$$

---

## Final Result

Distance to the cliff:

$$
d = 171.5 \text{ m}
$$

---

## Interpretation

The sound travels to the cliff and back, covering twice the distance. Dividing the total distance by 2 gives the actual distance to the cliff.

The echo delay allows accurate determination of distances using the known speed of sound.
