# Solutions goes here


Given:
v₀ = 100 m/s  
θ = 37°  
g = 9.81 m/s²  

---

First, we split the velocity:

v₀x = v₀ cosθ = 100 cos37 ≈ 79.86 m/s  
v₀y = v₀ sinθ = 100 sin37 ≈ 60.18 m/s  

---

Equations of motion:

Horizontal:
d²x/dt² = 0 → x(t) = v₀x t  

Vertical:
d²y/dt² = -g → y(t) = v₀y t - (1/2)gt²  

---

Time of flight:

T = 2v₀y / g  
T = 2(60.18) / 9.81 ≈ 12.27 s  

---

Maximum height:

H = v₀y² / (2g)  
H = (60.18)² / (2·9.81) ≈ 184.6 m  

---

Range:

R = v₀x · T  
R = 79.86 · 12.27 ≈ 979.7 m  

---

Final answers:

T ≈ 12.27 s  
H ≈ 184.6 m  
R ≈ 979.7 m
