We use centripetal acceleration formula:

a = v^2 / r

For a person standing on the equator, the speed comes from Earth's rotation.

---

Earth radius:

r = 6378 km = 6.378 × 10^6 m

Earth period:

T = 24 h = 86400 s

---

First find speed:

v = 2πr / T

v = 2π(6.378 × 10^6) / 86400

v ≈ 463.8 m/s

---

Now calculate centripetal acceleration:

a = v^2 / r

a = (463.8)^2 / (6.378 × 10^6)

a ≈ 0.0337 m/s^2

---

Final answer:

a ≈ 0.034 m/s^2
