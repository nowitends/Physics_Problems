Momentum formula:

p = mv

---

Fly:

m = 2 g = 0.002 kg  
v = 10 m/s  

p = 0.002 × 10 = 0.02 kg·m/s

---

Tennis ball:

m = 60 g = 0.06 kg  
v = 1 m/s  

p = 0.06 × 1 = 0.06 kg·m/s

---

Final answer:

Fly momentum = 0.02 kg·m/s  
Tennis ball momentum = 0.06 kg·m/s  

So the **60-gram tennis ball** has greater momentum.
