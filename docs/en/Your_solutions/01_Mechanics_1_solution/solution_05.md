Given:

river speed = 2 m/s east  
boat speed in still water = 5 m/s  
river width = 200 m  

The boat wants to go directly north, so the west component of the boat must cancel the river flow.

---

Let the heading angle be \(\theta\) west of north.

Then:

\[
5\sin\theta = 2
\]

\[
\sin\theta = \frac{2}{5}
\]

\[
\theta = \sin^{-1}(0.4) \approx 23.6^\circ
\]

So the boat should head about \(23.6^\circ\) west of north.

---

Now find the north component of velocity:

\[
v_y = 5\cos\theta
\]

\[
v_y = 5\cos 23.6^\circ \approx 4.58 \text{ m/s}
\]

---

Time to cross:

\[
t = \frac{200}{4.58} \approx 43.7 \text{ s}
\]

---

Final answer:

Heading angle \(\approx 23.6^\circ\) west of north  
Crossing time \(\approx 43.7\) s
