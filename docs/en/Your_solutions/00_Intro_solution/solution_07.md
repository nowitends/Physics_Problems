## 7. Logic & Series

A bicycle is 10 meters from a wall and moves toward it at a constant speed of 1 m/s.

A fly starts from the bicycle and flies toward the wall at a constant speed of 2 m/s, turning back instantly each time it reaches the wall or the bicycle.

We need the total distance traveled by the fly before the bicycle reaches the wall.

---

### Step 1 — Time until the bicycle reaches the wall

The bicycle is 10 meters away and moves at 1 m/s.

\[
t = \frac{d}{v} = \frac{10}{1} = 10 \text{ s}
\]

So the bicycle reaches the wall after 10 seconds.

---

### Step 2 — Distance traveled by the fly

The fly moves during the whole 10 seconds at a speed of 2 m/s.

\[
d = vt = 2 \cdot 10 = 20 \text{ m}
\]

---

### Final Answer

The total distance traveled by the fly is:

\[
20 \text{ m}
\]
