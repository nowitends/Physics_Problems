## 9. Optimization Problem

A rectangle is under the curve

\[
y = 3 - x^2
\]

in the first quadrant.

We want to find the dimensions of the rectangle with maximum area.

---

### Step 1 — Write the area formula

If the top-right corner of the rectangle is at \((x, y)\), then

\[
y = 3 - x^2
\]

So the area is

\[
A(x) = x \cdot y = x(3 - x^2)
\]

\[
A(x) = 3x - x^3
\]

---

### Step 2 — Differentiate

\[
A'(x) = 3 - 3x^2
\]

Set the derivative equal to zero:

\[
3 - 3x^2 = 0
\]

\[
1 - x^2 = 0
\]

\[
x^2 = 1
\]

\[
x = 1
\]

Since we are in the first quadrant, we take \(x=1\).

---

### Step 3 — Find y

\[
y = 3 - x^2 = 3 - 1 = 2
\]

---

### Final Answer

The rectangle with maximum area has dimensions:

\[
1 \times 2
\]

So:

- width = \(1\)
- height = \(2\)
