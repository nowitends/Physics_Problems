## 2. Systems of Equations

We are given the system:

2x + 3y = 12  
x − y = 1  

From the second equation:

x = y + 1

Substitute into the first equation:

2(y + 1) + 3y = 12  

2y + 2 + 3y = 12  

5y + 2 = 12  

5y = 10  

y = 2  

Now substitute back:

x = y + 1  
x = 2 + 1  
x = 3  

### Final Answer

x = 3  
y = 2
