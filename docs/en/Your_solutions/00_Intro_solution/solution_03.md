## 3. Proportionality

The Universal Law of Gravitation is

F = G (m1 m2) / r²

We analyze how the force changes when:

- distance r is doubled
- both masses are halved

---

### Step 1 — Effect of doubling distance

If r becomes 2r:

F ∝ 1 / (2r)²

F ∝ 1 / 4r²

So the force becomes:

F → F / 4

---

### Step 2 — Effect of halving both masses

If

m1 → m1/2  
m2 → m2/2

then

m1 m2 → (m1/2)(m2/2)

m1 m2 → m1 m2 / 4

So the force becomes:

F → F / 4

---

### Step 3 — Combine both effects

Distance change → F / 4  
Mass change → F / 4

Total change:

F → (1/4) × (1/4)

F → F / 16

---

### Final Answer

The gravitational force becomes **1/16 of the original force**.
