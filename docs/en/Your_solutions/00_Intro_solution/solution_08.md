## 8. Definite Integrals

We need to calculate the area under the curve of the function

\[
f(x) = \sin(x)
\]

from

\[
x = 0 \quad \text{to} \quad x = \pi
\]

---

### Step 1 — Write the definite integral

\[
\int_0^\pi \sin(x)\,dx
\]

---

### Step 2 — Find the antiderivative

The antiderivative of \(\sin(x)\) is:

\[
-\cos(x)
\]

So,

\[
\int_0^\pi \sin(x)\,dx = \left[-\cos(x)\right]_0^\pi
\]

---

### Step 3 — Evaluate at the limits

\[
-\cos(\pi) - \left(-\cos(0)\right)
\]

Since

\[
\cos(\pi) = -1, \qquad \cos(0) = 1
\]

we get

\[
-(-1) - (-1) = 1 + 1 = 2
\]

---

### Final Answer

The area under the curve is:

\[
2
\]
