## 5. Trigonometry

A vector \( \vec{A} \) has magnitude \( 15 \) and makes an angle of \( 60^\circ \) with the horizontal axis.

We find its components using:

\[
A_x = A\cos\theta
\]

\[
A_y = A\sin\theta
\]

Substitute the values:

\[
A_x = 15\cos 60^\circ = 15 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 7.5
\]

\[
A_y = 15\sin 60^\circ = 15 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{15\sqrt{3}}{2}
\]

Approximate vertical component:

\[
A_y \approx 12.99
\]

### Final Answer

\[
A_x = 7.5, \qquad A_y = \frac{15\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx 12.99
\]
