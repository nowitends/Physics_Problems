# Task 08 – Lorentz Force

## Problem Statement

A charged particle has:

* $q = 2 \times 10^{-19} , \text{C}$
* $m = 4 \times 10^{-27} , \text{kg}$
* $v = 10^6 , \text{m/s}$
* $B = 0.5 , \text{T}$

The velocity is perpendicular to the magnetic field.

Determine the magnitude of the Lorentz force.

---

## Theory

### Lorentz Force in a Magnetic Field

When a charged particle moves in a magnetic field, the magnetic part of the Lorentz force is:

$$
F = qvB \sin\theta
$$

For perpendicular motion:

$$
\theta = 90^\circ
$$

Thus:

$$
\sin 90^\circ = 1
$$

So the formula reduces to:

$$
F = qvB
$$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Substitute Values

$$
F = (2 \times 10^{-19})(10^6)(0.5)
$$

---

## Step 2: Multiply Numerical Parts

First multiply constants:

$$
2 \times 0.5 = 1
$$

So:

$$
F = 1 \times 10^{-19} \times 10^6
$$

---

## Step 3: Combine Powers of 10

$$
10^{-19} \times 10^6 = 10^{-13}
$$

Thus:

$$
F = 10^{-13} , \text{N}
$$

---

## Final Result

$$
F = 1.0 \times 10^{-13} , \text{N}
$$

---

## Interpretation

The Lorentz force acts perpendicular to the velocity and magnetic field.

Key observations:

* It does not change the speed of the particle
* It only changes the direction of motion
* This force is responsible for circular or helical trajectories in magnetic fields

The magnitude depends directly on charge, velocity, and magnetic field strength.
