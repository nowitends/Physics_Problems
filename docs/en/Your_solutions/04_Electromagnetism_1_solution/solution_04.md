# Task 04 – Comparison of Electric and Gravitational Forces

## Problem Statement

Calculate:

* The electric force between an electron and a proton
* The gravitational force between them

Given:

$$
r = 5.3 \times 10^{-11} , \text{m}
$$

Determine the ratio:

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g}
$$

---

## Theory

### Electric Force (Coulomb’s Law)

$$
F_e = k \frac{e^2}{r^2}
$$

where:

* $k = 9.0 \times 10^9 , \text{N m}^2/\text{C}^2$
* $e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19} , \text{C}$

---

### Gravitational Force (Newton’s Law)

$$
F_g = G \frac{m_e m_p}{r^2}
$$

where:

* $G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11} , \text{N m}^2/\text{kg}^2$
* $m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31} , \text{kg}$
* $m_p = 1.67 \times 10^{-27} , \text{kg}$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Electric Force Calculation

$$
F_e = 9.0 \times 10^9 \cdot \frac{(1.6 \times 10^{-19})^2}{(5.3 \times 10^{-11})^2}
$$

Square the charge:

$$
(1.6 \times 10^{-19})^2 = 2.56 \times 10^{-38}
$$

Square the distance:

$$
(5.3 \times 10^{-11})^2 = 2.809 \times 10^{-21}
$$

Substitute:

$$
F_e = 9.0 \times 10^9 \cdot \frac{2.56 \times 10^{-38}}{2.809 \times 10^{-21}}
$$

$$
F_e = 9.0 \times 10^9 \cdot 9.11 \times 10^{-18}
$$

$$
F_e \approx 8.2 \times 10^{-8} , \text{N}
$$

---

### Step 2: Gravitational Force Calculation

$$
F_g = 6.67 \times 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(1.67 \times 10^{-27})}{(5.3 \times 10^{-11})^2}
$$

Multiply masses:

$$
(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(1.67 \times 10^{-27}) = 1.52 \times 10^{-57}
$$

Substitute:

$$
F_g = 6.67 \times 10^{-11} \cdot \frac{1.52 \times 10^{-57}}{2.809 \times 10^{-21}}
$$

$$
F_g = 6.67 \times 10^{-11} \cdot 5.41 \times 10^{-37}
$$

$$
F_g \approx 3.6 \times 10^{-47} , \text{N}
$$

---

### Step 3: Ratio of Forces

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} = \frac{8.2 \times 10^{-8}}{3.6 \times 10^{-47}}
$$

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{39}
$$

---

## Final Result

Electric force:

$$
F_e \approx 8.2 \times 10^{-8} , \text{N}
$$

Gravitational force:

$$
F_g \approx 3.6 \times 10^{-47} , \text{N}
$$

Ratio:

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{39}
$$

---

## Interpretation

The electric force between the electron and proton is approximately $10^{39}$ times stronger than the gravitational force.

This demonstrates:

* Gravity is negligible at atomic scales
* Electromagnetic interaction dominates atomic structure

This is why atoms are bound by electric forces rather than gravitational attraction.
