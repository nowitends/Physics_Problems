# Task 05 – Electric Field Required for Proton Levitation

## Problem Statement

Determine the electric field strength required to make a proton levitate against Earth's gravity.

Given:

* $m_p = 1.67 \times 10^{-27} , \text{kg}$
* $e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19} , \text{C}$
* $g = 9.8 , \text{m/s}^2$

---

## Theory

### Forces Acting on the Proton

Two forces act on the proton:

1. Gravitational force (downward)

$$
F_g = m g
$$

2. Electric force (direction depends on field)

$$
F_e = qE
$$

---

### Condition for Levitation

For levitation (equilibrium):

$$
F_e = F_g
$$

Thus,

$$
qE = mg
$$

Solve for electric field:

$$
E = \frac{mg}{q}
$$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Substitute Known Values

$$
E = \frac{(1.67 \times 10^{-27})(9.8)}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}
$$

---

### Step 2: Multiply Numerator

$$
1.67 \times 9.8 = 16.366
$$

$$
E = \frac{16.366 \times 10^{-27}}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}}
$$

---

### Step 3: Simplify Powers of 10

$$
\frac{10^{-27}}{10^{-19}} = 10^{-8}
$$

Thus,

$$
E = \frac{16.366}{1.6} \times 10^{-8}
$$

---

### Step 4: Final Calculation

$$
\frac{16.366}{1.6} \approx 10.23
$$

$$
E \approx 10.23 \times 10^{-8}
$$

$$
E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7} , \text{N/C}
$$

---

## Final Result

$$
E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7} , \text{N/C}
$$

Direction:

* The electric field must be **upward** so that the electric force on the positive proton acts upward.

---

## Interpretation

The required electric field is extremely small compared to typical electric fields.

This result shows:

* Gravity is extremely weak at the particle scale
* Even a very small electric field can counteract gravitational force on a proton

This reinforces the dominance of electromagnetic effects over gravity at microscopic scales.
