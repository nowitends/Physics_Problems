# Task 10 – Magnetic Force on a Current-Carrying Wire

## Problem Statement

A straight wire has:

* Length $L = 2.0 , \text{m}$
* Current $I = 10 , \text{A}$
* Magnetic field $B = 0.5 , \text{T}$

Find the magnetic force for:

a) $90^\circ$
b) $45^\circ$
c) $0^\circ$

---

## Theory

### Force on a Current-Carrying Wire

A current-carrying conductor in a magnetic field experiences a force:

$$
F = B I L \sin\theta
$$

where:

* $B$ = magnetic field strength
* $I$ = current
* $L$ = length of wire in field
* $\theta$ = angle between wire and magnetic field

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

## Step 1: Common Factor

Compute the constant part:

$$
BIL = (0.5)(10)(2.0)
$$

$$
BIL = 10
$$

So:

$$
F = 10 \sin\theta
$$

---

## Part (a): $\theta = 90^\circ$

$$
\sin 90^\circ = 1
$$

$$
F = 10 \times 1
$$

$$
F = 10 , \text{N}
$$

---

## Part (b): $\theta = 45^\circ$

$$
\sin 45^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
$$

$$
F = 10 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
$$

$$
F = 5\sqrt{2}
$$

$$
F \approx 7.07 , \text{N}
$$

---

## Part (c): $\theta = 0^\circ$

$$
\sin 0^\circ = 0
$$

$$
F = 0
$$

---

## Final Results

$$
F_{90^\circ} = 10 , \text{N}
$$

$$
F_{45^\circ} = 5\sqrt{2} \approx 7.07 , \text{N}
$$

$$
F_{0^\circ} = 0 , \text{N}
$$

---

## Interpretation

* Maximum force occurs when wire is perpendicular to field
* Force decreases as angle decreases
* No force occurs when wire is parallel to magnetic field

This follows directly from the $\sin\theta$ dependence in the Lorentz force for current-carrying conductors.
