# Task 01 – Electric Force at the Center of a Square Charge Configuration

## Problem Statement

Four point charges of $+1.0 , \text{C}$ are placed at the corners of a square of side $1.0 , \text{m}$. A charge of $-2.0 , \text{C}$ is placed at the center of the square.

Determine the **magnitude** and **direction** of the net electric force acting on the charge.

---

## Theory

### Coulomb’s Law

The magnitude of the electric force between two point charges is given by

$$
F = k \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}
$$

where:

* $k = 9.0 \times 10^9 , \text{N m}^2/\text{C}^2$
* $q_1, q_2$ are the charges
* $r$ is the separation distance

---

### Symmetry Principle

For symmetric charge distributions:

* Forces may cancel due to equal magnitude and opposite direction
* Vector addition is essential

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Geometry of the Square

Side length:

$$
a = 1.0 , \text{m}
$$

Distance from center to any corner:

$$
r = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} , \text{m}
$$

---

### Step 2: Force Due to One Corner Charge

Using Coulomb’s Law:

$$
F = k \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}
$$

Substitute values:

$$
F = 9.0 \times 10^9 \cdot \frac{(1)(2)}{\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2}
$$

$$
F = 9.0 \times 10^9 \cdot \frac{2}{\frac{1}{2}}
$$

$$
F = 9.0 \times 10^9 \cdot 4
$$

$$
F = 3.6 \times 10^{10} , \text{N}
$$

Each corner exerts a force of this magnitude toward itself (attractive force since the charge is negative).

---

### Step 3: Direction of Forces

Each force acts along a diagonal from the center toward a corner.

There are four such forces:

* Equal in magnitude
* Symmetrically distributed
* Opposite pairs cancel each other

---

### Step 4: Vector Cancellation

Consider opposite corners:

* Their forces are equal and opposite
* Net force along each diagonal = $0$

Thus, summing all four forces:

$$
\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = 0
$$

---

## Final Result

Magnitude of the net force:

$$
F_{\text{net}} = 0 , \text{N}
$$

Direction:

Undefined (no net force)

---

## Interpretation

The charge experiences equal  forces from all four corners. Due to perfect symmetry, these forces cancel exactly.

This configuration represents a point of **electrostatic equilibrium**, where the net force is zero despite strong individual interactions.
